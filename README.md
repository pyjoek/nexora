# Nexora — HR Core Workforce Platform

Nexora is an all-in-one HR Core system built for companies that need to manage, monitor, and maintain full control of their workforce. From work permit tracking to payroll processing and role-based access control, Nexora centralizes every critical HR function into a single, unified platform.

---

## Overview

Managing a large or diverse workforce comes with real complexity — staff records scattered across systems, permit expiry dates missed, payroll errors, shift conflicts, and employees accessing data they shouldn't. Nexora was built to solve all of that. It gives HR teams, managers, and executives a single source of truth for everything workforce-related, while ensuring every user only sees what their role permits them to see.

---

## Key Features

**Employee Management**
Maintain complete employee profiles including personal details, employment contracts, documents, department assignments, and full employment history. Search, filter, and manage your entire workforce from one place.

**Work Permit & Compliance Tracking**
Track visas, work permits, and residency documents for every employee. Nexora automatically flags upcoming expiry dates and sends renewal alerts so your company stays compliant at all times.

**Payroll Processing**
Automate salary calculations, deductions, allowances, and bonus processing. Payslips are generated automatically at the end of each payroll cycle. Payroll data is directly linked to attendance records to ensure accuracy.

**Roster Management**
Plan and publish shift rosters for teams and departments. Managers can assign shifts, handle leave requests, and adjust schedules in real time. Employees can view their own roster based on their access level.

**Attendance Monitoring**
Track daily check-ins, absences, late arrivals, and overtime. Attendance data feeds directly into payroll calculations and can be exported as reports for any date range or department.

**Role-Based Access Control**
Every user in Nexora is assigned a role, and every role is governed by an access policy. This means each user sees only the modules, data, and actions they are permitted to access — nothing more. Access is fully configurable by administrators.

---

## How It Works

1. **Onboard Your Organization** — Set up your company profile, departments, locations, and branches. Import existing employee records or add staff manually.

2. **Configure Roles & Policies** — Define user roles and configure access policies for each module. Assign roles to staff so every user gets the right level of access from day one.

3. **Run Daily HR Operations** — Use the dashboard to manage attendance, prepare rosters, process payroll, and monitor work permit statuses all in one place.

4. **Report & Stay Compliant** — Generate reports across any module, track permit renewals, and maintain audit-ready records for your workforce at all times.

